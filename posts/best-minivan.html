Road Trip Ready: How to Choose the Best Minivan for Weekend Getawayse

Dec 1, 2025 • By Canopy

Planning a quick escape from the city? Whether you're heading to the serene gardens of Epe Resort or taking a family drive to see the sights at Olumo Rock, the vehicle you choose makes all the difference. While SUVs get a lot of love, the minivan remains the undisputed king of the weekend road trip.

But with so many options on the market, how do you pick the right "home on wheels"? Here is a guide to choosing the best minivan for your next weekend getaway.

1. Prioritize Seating Flexibility

The magic of a minivan lies in its interior. Look for models with stowable or removable seating.
  • The Stow ‘n Go Advantage: Some vans allow the second and third rows to fold completely flat into the floor. This is a game-changer if you need to switch from hauling seven people to carrying camping gear or a cooler in seconds.
  • Captain’s Chairs: If comfort is the priority for your adult friends or older kids, look for second-row captain’s chairs which offer more personal space and easier access to the back.

2. Fuel Efficiency (Go Hybrid if Possible)

With fuel costs being a major factor for any traveler, efficiency is key. Hybrid models not only save you money at the pump but often provide a smoother, quieter ride,perfect for when the kids finally fall asleep in the back.

  • The Toyota Sienna (a local favorite in Nigeria for its reliability) now comes exclusively as a hybrid, offering incredible mileage for long stretches of road.

3. Safety and Driver-Assist Tech

A weekend getaway should be relaxing, not stressful. Ensure your choice includes modern safety features:

  • Adaptive Cruise Control: Great for maintaining a safe distance on the highway.
  • Blind Spot Monitoring: Essential for a vehicle of this size.
  • 360-Degree Cameras: These make parking in tight resort spots or navigating busy markets much easier.

4. Entertainment for the Long Haul

A "best" minivan must keep the passengers happy. Look for:

  • Built-in Wi-Fi Hotspots: Keep everyone connected for navigation or streaming.
  • Rear Entertainment Systems: High-definition screens with HDMI ports for gaming consoles (perfect for a quick round of Free Fire during a rest stop).
  • Multiple USB Ports: Ensure there are enough charging points in every row so no one’s phone dies mid-trip.

5. Storage and Cargo Solutions

Check the trunk depth. Some minivans have a deep cargo well behind the third row, which is perfect for stacking suitcases vertically. Also, look for roof rail compatibility if you plan on bringing bikes or a rooftop carrier.

Top Picks for 2026

  • Toyota Sienna: The reliability king. Best for fuel economy and long-term value.
  • Honda Odyssey: Known for the best handling; it drives more like a car than a bus.
  • Kia Carnival: The "MPV" that looks like a sleek SUV but offers massive interior space.
  • Chrysler Pacifica: The leader in interior luxury and seating versatility..
